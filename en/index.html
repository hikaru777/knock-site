01 / HOW IT WORKS

Rest on the edge. Choose.
Bump three times.

  1. RestRest your cursor on the right edge and a dial of friends appears. Scroll up and down to choose a person, sideways to choose a group.
  2. KnockBump the edge three times and they get a knock. Answer and Just a moment appear on the edge of their screen.
  3. RoomWhen they press Answer, a room opens on the edge of both screens.

02 / IN THE ROOM

Lighter than talking.
Closer than chat.

Their cursor

In the room, their cursor moves with their name on it. Pointing is enough.

Quick replies

“OK”, “Wait”, “Later” — without typing. Messages too.

Show your screen

Show just the window you choose and pin a spot on it. Several screens line up in the room: click to enlarge, drag one out to give it its own window.

Hand over files

Drop a file into the room and it arrives on their Mac.

Groups

Share one room with several friends. Scroll the dial sideways to pick a group.

Focus

While you focus, knocks wait quietly.

03 / PRIVACY

Cursors and screens go
between your two Macs.

Cursors, quick replies, shared screens and files travel over a direct, encrypted connection between the two Macs. Only when a direct connection can’t be made does the server relay them, live, without storing them.

The server handles sign-in, knocks, messages, setting up the direct connection, and delivery while a friend is offline. There are no ad or analytics tools. Privacy policy (Japanese)

04 / DOWNLOAD

Install it together
with a friend.

  1. Open Knock.zip and move Knock to your Applications folder.
  2. Open Knock and sign in with the six-digit code sent to your email.
  3. Choose a name and send your invite code to a friend. Once they enter it, you’re connected.
